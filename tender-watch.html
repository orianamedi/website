Slovenske bolnišnice kupujejo prek okvirnih sporazumov in dinamičnih nabavnih sistemov. Kdor zamudi vstop, čaka leta.

Slovenian hospitals buy through framework agreements and dynamic purchasing systems. Miss the entry point and you wait for years.

Slovensko javno zdravstvo (univerzitetna klinična centra, Onkološki inštitut, splošne bolnišnice, javni zdravstveni domovi, javni domovi za starejše in druge javne zdravstvene ustanove) medicinske pripomočke praviloma kupuje prek večletnih okvirnih sporazumov in vse pogosteje prek dinamičnih nabavnih sistemov (DNS). Pri okvirnem sporazumu se konkurenca med izbranimi ponudniki praviloma ponovno odpira, sodelujejo pa lahko le tisti, ki jim je bila priznana sposobnost že na prvem razpisu. Pri DNS je treba najprej pridobiti priznanje sposobnosti (zanj je mogoče zaprositi tudi med trajanjem sistema), nato naročnik po potrebi razpisuje posamezne manjše sklope. Razpisi se na portalu eNaročanje pogosto pojavijo le nekaj tednov pred rokom in so v slovenščini.

Slovenian public healthcare (university medical centres, the Institute of Oncology, general hospitals, public health centres, public care homes for the elderly and other public health institutions) usually buys medical devices through multi-year framework agreements and, increasingly, through dynamic purchasing systems (DPS). Under a framework agreement, competition is typically reopened only among the suppliers admitted in the original tender. Under a DPS, you first have to be admitted (you can apply at any time while the system runs), and the buyer then tenders individual smaller product groups as needed. Tenders on the national eNaročanje portal often appear only a few weeks before the deadline, and only in Slovenian.

Good to know before we start

Spremljanje po najboljših močeh

Spremljamo skrbno, a ne odgovarjamo za napake, izpuste ali zamude objav na portalu in ne moremo jamčiti izida razpisa.

En proizvajalec na sklop

Nikoli ne prijavimo dveh proizvajalcev v isti sklop. Če sklop ustreza več strankam, ga dobi prvo potrjeno naročilo prijave.

Fiksne cene, pisno potrjene

Prijave na razpise običajno veljajo do 180 dni, cene pa so fiksne za čas trajanja okvirnega sporazuma in pogodb, sklenjenih na njegovi podlagi (kot določa razpisna dokumentacija). Pred oddajo pisno potrdite fiksno transferno ceno v evrih ali se dogovorimo o delitvi valutnega tveganja.

Garancije

Garancije za resnost ponudbe in dobro izvedbo (bančna garancija ali menica) izdamo mi kot ponudnik, stroške prenesemo po nabavni ceni. Če je garancija unovčena zaradi izpada dobave na strani proizvajalca, jo krije proizvajalec.

Ali potrebujemo distribucijsko pogodbo?

Za Tender Watch in pripravo prijav na razpise ne; zadošča pooblastilo za posamezen razpis. Če želite stalno prisotnost na trgu, vaše izdelke lahko prevzamemo tudi v distribucijo.

Best-efforts monitoring

We monitor with care, but are not liable for errors, omissions or delays in portal publications, and cannot guarantee the outcome of any tender.

One manufacturer per product group

We never bid for two manufacturers in the same product group of a tender. If a product group fits more than one client, the first confirmed bid order takes it.

Fixed prices, confirmed in writing

Bids usually stay valid for up to 180 days and prices stay fixed for the term of the framework agreement and the contracts concluded under it (as set out in the tender documents). Before we bid, you confirm a fixed transfer price in euros in writing, or we agree how currency risk is shared.

Guarantees

Bid security and performance guarantees (bank guarantee or bill of exchange) are issued by us as the bidder, at cost. If a guarantee is called because of a supply failure on the manufacturer’s side, the manufacturer covers it.

Do we need a distribution agreement?

Not for Tender Watch or bid preparation; an authorisation for each tender is enough. If you want a permanent market presence, we can also take your products into distribution.
