Slovenian hospitals buy through framework agreements. Most reopen once a year.

Slovensko javno zdravstvo (dva univerzitetna klinična centra, Onkološki inštitut in mreža splošnih bolnišnic) medicinske pripomočke praviloma kupuje prek večletnih okvirnih sporazumov, ki se večinoma ponovno odprejo približno vsakih 12 mesecev. Ustrezen razpis se na portalu eNaročanje pogosto pojavi le nekaj tednov pred rokom, v slovenščini, in se prav tako hitro zapre. Zamujen razpis lahko pomeni leto čakanja.

Slovenian public healthcare (two university medical centres, the Institute of Oncology and the general hospital network) typically procures medical devices through multi-year framework agreements, most reopened for competition about every 12 months. A relevant tender often appears on the national eNaročanje portal with only a few weeks’ notice, in Slovenian, and closes just as quickly. Missing it can mean waiting a year for the next opening.

Cene priprave ponudb so fiksne, znane pred začetkom dela in plačljive vnaprej; delo začnemo ob prejemu plačila. Plačila niso vračljiva, ne glede na izid razpisa. Stroške tretjih oseb (garancije, sodni prevodi, vzorci, kurir) prenesemo po nabavni ceni, brez pribitka.

Bid fees are fixed, quoted before work starts and payable in advance; work starts on receipt of payment. Bid fees are non-refundable, whatever the tender outcome. Third-party costs (guarantees, sworn translations, samples, courier) are passed through at cost, never marked up.

Pred oddajo potrebujemo pooblastilo proizvajalca za konkreten razpis in kratke dobavne pogoje: fiksno transferno ceno za obdobje pogodbe, dobavne roke in konsignacijsko zalogo.

Before we bid, we need a manufacturer’s authorisation letter for that specific tender and short supply terms: a fixed transfer price for the contract period, delivery times and consignment stock.

Če ponudba zmaga, sporazum dobavljamo kot vaš lokalni izvajalec, na dogovorjeno maržo ali provizijo, za celotno obdobje pogodbe. Sodelovanje lahko kadar koli nadgradimo v distribucijsko pogodbo.

If a bid wins, we supply the framework as your local operator, on an agreed margin or invoice commission, for the full contract period. The cooperation can be upgraded to a distribution agreement at any time.

Preprosto povedano: majhna mesečnina, da ne zamudite nobenega ustreznega razpisa, fiksno plačilo samo za razpise, za katere se odločite, na dobavah pa zaslužimo, ko skupaj zmagamo.

In plain terms: a small monthly fee to never miss a relevant tender, a fixed fee only for the tenders you choose to pursue, and we earn on deliveries when we win together.

Dobro je vedeti pred začetkom

Good to know before we start

Spremljanje po najboljših močeh

Spremljamo skrbno, a ne odgovarjamo za napake, izpuste ali zamude objav na portalu in ne moremo jamčiti izida razpisa.

En proizvajalec na sklop

Nikoli ne oddamo ponudbe za dva proizvajalca v istem sklopu. Če sklop ustreza več strankam, ga dobi prvo potrjeno naročilo ponudbe.

Fiksne cene, pisno potrjene

Ponudbe običajno veljajo do 180 dni, cene pa so fiksne za obdobje sporazuma. Pred oddajo pisno potrdite fiksno transferno ceno v evrih ali se dogovorimo o delitvi valutnega tveganja.

Garancije

Garancije za resnost ponudbe in dobro izvedbo (bančna garancija ali menica) izdamo mi kot ponudnik, stroške prenesemo po nabavni ceni. Če je garancija unovčena zaradi izpada dobave na strani proizvajalca, jo krije proizvajalec.

Ali potrebujemo distribucijsko pogodbo?

Za Tender Watch in pripravo ponudb ne; zadošča pooblastilo za posamezen razpis. Če želite stalno prisotnost na trgu, vaše izdelke lahko prevzamemo tudi v distribucijo.

Best-efforts monitoring

We monitor with care, but are not liable for errors, omissions or delays in portal publications, and cannot guarantee the outcome of any tender.

One manufacturer per lot

We never bid for two manufacturers in the same lot. If a lot fits more than one client, the first confirmed bid order takes it.

Fixed prices, confirmed in writing

Bids usually stay valid for up to 180 days and prices stay fixed for the framework period. Before we bid, you confirm a fixed transfer price in euros in writing, or we agree how currency risk is shared.

Guarantees

Bid security and performance guarantees (bank guarantee or bill of exchange) are issued by us as the bidder, at cost. If a guarantee is called because of a supply failure on the manufacturer’s side, the manufacturer covers it.

Do we need a distribution agreement?

Not for Tender Watch or bid preparation; an authorisation for each tender is enough. If you want a permanent market presence, we can also take your products into distribution.
