Politika zasebnosti

Katere podatke zbiramo

Prek kontaktnega obrazca: ime in priimek, podjetje ali ustanovo, e-naslov, telefon (neobvezno), izbrano temo in vsebino sporočila. Spletna stran si v vašem brskalniku zapomni le izbrani jezik (lokalna shramba); piškotkov za sledenje ali oglaševanje ne uporabljamo.

Namen in pravna podlaga

Podatke uporabimo izključno za odgovor na vaše povpraševanje in za morebitno pripravo ponudbe. Pravna podlaga je vaša privolitev (točka (a) člena 6(1) Splošne uredbe o varstvu podatkov) in ukrepi pred sklenitvijo pogodbe na vašo zahtevo (točka (b)).

Obdelovalci

Sporočila iz obrazca nam posreduje zunanja storitev FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), ki jih pošlje na naš e-naslov. Pisave spletne strani nalaga storitev Google Fonts.

Hramba

Povpraševanja hranimo, dokler je to potrebno za odgovor in morebitno nadaljnje poslovno sodelovanje, najdlje pa 2 leti od zadnjega stika, razen če zakon zahteva daljšo hrambo.

Vaše pravice

Pravico imate do dostopa, popravka, izbrisa, omejitve obdelave, prenosljivosti podatkov in ugovora ter do preklica privolitve kadar koli, s sporočilom na info@orianamedi.com. Pritožbo lahko vložite pri Informacijskem pooblaščencu Republike Slovenije (www.ip-rs.si).

Privacy policy

Controller: Oriana Medi d.o.o., Celjska cesta 47, 3252 Rogatec, Slovenia, reg. no. 9944800000, email: info@orianamedi.com.

What we collect

Through the contact form: your name, company or institution, email, phone (optional), the selected topic and your message. The website remembers only your chosen language in your browser (local storage); we use no tracking or advertising cookies.

Purpose and legal basis

We use the data only to reply to your enquiry and, where relevant, to prepare an offer. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).

Processors

Form messages are forwarded to our email address by the external service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). The site’s fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Retention

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to reply and for any follow-up business, and no longer than 2 years after our last contact, unless the law requires longer retention.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and may withdraw consent at any time by writing to info@orianamedi.com. You may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia (www.ip-rs.si).
