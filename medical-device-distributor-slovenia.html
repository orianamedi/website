How Slovenian hospitals buy medical devices

Almost every hospital purchase in Slovenia goes through public procurement. The rules are EU rules, but the practice is local: the language, the portal, the buyers and the timing.

Tenders are published in SlovenianOn the national e-procurement portal, and many fall below the EU threshold, so they never appear on TED. Bid forms and the ESPD must be completed in Slovenian.
Framework agreements run for yearsOften around 36 months. A win means supply revenue for years; missing the first tender usually means waiting for the next agreement.
Dynamic purchasing systemsSuppliers qualify first, which is possible during the system’s lifetime, and then compete in smaller calls for specific product groups.
A local registered bidder mattersTenders typically require the bidder to be registered with JAZMP as a medical device distributor, with local delivery, consignment and complaint handling.
Foreign origin is not a barrierCE-marked products from any country compete on equal terms. Technical documentation in English is usually accepted.
The buyersUMC Ljubljana, UMC Maribor, the Institute of Oncology Ljubljana, general hospitals and health centres, many of them buying through joint procurement.

Selling medical devices in Slovenia: common questions

Do I need a local distributor to sell medical devices in Slovenia?

For EU-based manufacturers there is no legal requirement to appoint one. In practice, Slovenian hospital tenders typically require a bidder registered with JAZMP as a medical device distributor, bid forms in Slovenian submitted through the national e-procurement portal, and local delivery, consignment stock and complaint handling. A local importer and distributor covers all of this.

How do Slovenian hospitals buy medical devices?

Mostly through public tenders published in Slovenian on the national e-procurement portal. Many hospitals buy through framework agreements, often running around 36 months, and through dynamic purchasing systems, where suppliers qualify first and then compete in smaller calls during the system’s lifetime. Many tenders fall below the EU threshold and never appear on TED.

What is JAZMP?

JAZMP is the Agency for Medicinal Products and Medical Devices of the Republic of Slovenia, the national competent authority for medical devices. Oriana Medi d.o.o. is registered with JAZMP as both importer and distributor of medical devices.

Can you work with us without a distribution agreement?

Yes. Besides distribution, we offer public tender services: we monitor Slovenian tenders for your products and bid on the ones you choose as your local bidder, with a manufacturer’s authorisation for each tender.

Do you also work for our competitors?

We never bid for two manufacturers in the same product group of a tender. If a product group fits more than one client, it goes to whoever confirms the bid order first.

Which manufacturers are the best fit?

Manufacturers of CE-marked devices for vascular interventions (interventional cardiology, interventional radiology, neuroradiology and carotid procedures) and of sharps-safety products. For other device categories we can help you find the right Slovenian distributor.
