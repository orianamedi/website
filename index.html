Preventiva: manj poškodb z ostrimi predmeti

Prevention: fewer sharps injuries

Distribuiramo izdelke Qlicksmart, preproste pripomočke, s katerimi osebje v operacijskih dvoranah, na oddelkih in v lekarnah varno odstrani rezilo skalpela ali odpre ampulo, brez stika prstov z ostrim robom.

We distribute Qlicksmart products: simple devices that let staff in operating theatres, wards and pharmacies remove a scalpel blade or open an ampoule safely, without fingers touching the sharp edge.

Obveznost delodajalcaAn employer's duty Direktiva Sveta 2010/32/EU od zdravstvenih ustanov zahteva, da ocenijo tveganje poškodb z ostrimi predmeti in ga z ustreznimi ukrepi in pripomočki zmanjšajo. Council Directive 2010/32/EU requires healthcare employers to assess the risk of sharps injuries and reduce it with appropriate measures and devices.

Za proizvajalce: zakaj odlični pripomočki ne pridejo v slovenske bolnišnice

For manufacturers: why excellent devices don't reach Slovenian hospitals

Vaš izdelek ima oznako CE, klinične podatke in dobro ceno. Na razpisih v drugih državah zmagujete. V Sloveniji pa posel znova dobi nekdo drug. Redko je kriv izdelek. Krivo je vse okoli njega:

Your product is CE-marked, clinically proven and competitively priced. You win tenders elsewhere in Europe. In Slovenia, the contract goes to someone else. Again. It’s rarely the product. It’s everything around it:

Razpis je v slovenščini

The tender is in Slovenian

Objavljen je na nacionalnem portalu, ki ga vaša izvozna ekipa ne spremlja, pogosto pod pragom EU, zato ga na TED sploh ne vidite.

Published on a national portal your export team doesn’t watch, often below the EU threshold, so it never shows up on TED.

Rok je že mimo

The deadline has already passed

Ko razpis pride do vas, ni več časa za dokumente, prevode in vzorce.

By the time the tender reaches you, there’s no time left for documents, translations and samples.

Specifikacije ne povedo vsega

Specifications don’t tell the whole story

Sklopi so združeni, zahteve pogosto pisane na obstoječega dobavitelja. Brez klinične izkušnje ne veste, kje imate realno možnost.

Lots are bundled and requirements often lean towards the incumbent. Without clinical experience, you can’t tell where you really have a chance.

Nihče lokalno ni na vaši strani

Nobody local is on your side

Ni registriranega ponudnika, ni dokumentacije v slovenščini, ni nikogar, ki pozna naročnike in zadnji krog razpisa.

No registered bidder, no Slovenian-language documents, no one who knows the buyers and the last tender round.

3 leta3 years

Okvirni sporazumi v slovenskih bolnišnicah pogosto trajajo okoli 36 mesecev. En zamujen razpis vas lahko za dolgo zaklene iz bolnišnice.

Framework agreements in Slovenian hospitals often run around 36 months. One missed tender can lock you out of a hospital for a long time.

Pogosta vprašanja

Questions we’re often asked

Ste distributer ali ponudnik storitev?

Oboje. Smo pri JAZMP registriran uvoznik in distributer in izdelke prodajamo na podlagi distribucijskih pogodb. Proizvajalcem, ki slovenski trg šele preizkušajo, ponujamo tudi storitve na javnih naročilih, s pooblastilom za posamezen razpis.

Ali delate tudi za naše konkurente?

Za isti sklop nikoli ne nastopamo z dvema proizvajalcema. Če sklop ustreza več našim strankam, ga dobi tista, ki prva potrdi naročilo ponudbe.

Ali se med razpisom pogovarjate z bolnišnico?

Ne, in tudi nihče drug se ne bi smel. Med odprtim postopkom gredo vsa vprašanja prek uradnega portala. Ta vprašanja pripravimo mi.

Kje kupim izdelke Qlicksmart?

Pri nas. Pišite nam prek obrazca ali na info@orianamedi.com; pripravimo predstavitev in ponudbo za vašo ustanovo.

Koliko stanejo vaše storitve?

EUDAMED pregled je brezplačen. Cene spremljanja razpisov in priprave ponudb vam pošljemo skupaj s ponudbo za proizvajalce. Pogoje distribucije dogovorimo individualno.

Are you a distributor or a service provider?

Both. We are a JAZMP-registered importer and distributor and sell products under distribution agreements. For manufacturers still testing the Slovenian market, we also offer public tender services, with an authorisation for each tender.

Do you also work for our competitors?

We never bid for two manufacturers in the same lot. If a lot fits more than one client, it goes to whoever confirms the bid order first.

Do you talk to the hospital during a tender?

No, and nobody should. During an open procedure, all questions go through the official portal. We prepare those questions for you.

Where can I buy Qlicksmart products?

From us. Use the form or write to info@orianamedi.com and we will prepare a demonstration and quote for your institution.

What do your services cost?

The EUDAMED check is free. We send prices for tender monitoring and bid preparation together with our manufacturer offer. Distribution terms are agreed individually.
